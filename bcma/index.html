How confidence and retention are calculated

Pair confidence /100: C = 50 × [native ipTM + max(ipSAEB→T, ipSAET→B)]. B = binder, T = target. Both inputs have equal weight. Native pTM and pLDDT are not included.

Across all five states: score = ½ × mean(C) + ½ × worst C. The directional ipSAE uses the maximum; the worst-state term still uses the minimum across antigens. One high-confidence pair cannot stand in for the other four. A per-antigen view ranks that pair’s C.

Predicted retention: count pairs meeting both metric cutoffs. The five states include WT, R27P, S30del, P34del and P33S. “5/5 evaluated” means five results exist; “5/5 retained” means all five pass the screen.

Sensitivity: the retention table shows counts at 85% and 95% of the same WT-control anchors, alongside the default 90% count. These are alternative screening margins, not confidence intervals. Cutoffs are frozen for this policy version.

Missing evidence and ties: across-state ranks require all five evaluations. Unknown pairs are never failures. Equal retention counts share ranks, without a confidence tiebreaker. Controls are excluded from contestant ranks; Fast and Boltz remain separate.

These are provisional model-based screens, not measured binding or affinities. No labeled nonbinder set is available here to estimate specificity. The retention screen and Red confidence-loss rule answer different questions; failing one is not a confirmed escape.

How provisional ranks are calculated

Blue: Per-pair confidence C = 50 × (native ipTM + maximum directional ipSAE). Overall = ½ mean(C) + ½ minimum(C), across all five states. All five must be present.

Red: After all 23 contestant WT scores are available, freeze an engine-specific panel with WT C ≥ 50. A signal requires ipTM and both directional ipSAE to each fall by at least the greater of 0.10 or 25% of the WT value. Rate = signals / fixed panel size. Rank only complete panel coverage.

These thresholds are an uncalibrated heuristic. WT C ≥ 50 does not establish binding; confidence loss does not establish target integrity or biological escape. WT and P33S remain unranked references. B5 and L10 are excluded from all contestant ranks. Equal six-decimal scores share competition ranks.

Engines are never averaged. Physics now has 123 accepted and two failed evaluations. The WT reference is incomplete, so combined ranks remain unavailable. Maximum-direction confidence also updates the WT C ≥ 50 Red eligibility panel; the three-metric decline criterion is unchanged.
